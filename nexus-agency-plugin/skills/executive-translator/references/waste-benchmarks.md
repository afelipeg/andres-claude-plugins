## How to Use in Executive Reports

### CEO Context
"Of your $X million investment, $Y million (Z%) reached consumers productively. The remaining $W million was absorbed by agency overhead, supply chain fees, quality issues, and targeting inefficiencies. This represents a $[recoverable] opportunity."

### CFO Context
"Current ROAS is X.Xx. If waste were eliminated, productive ROAS would be Y.Yx — a Z% improvement. Recovery roadmap identifies $[amount] in recoverable savings over [timeline]."

### CMO Context
"Media quality score: X/100. Working media ratio: Y%. Top waste categories: [ranked list]. Recommended actions: [prioritized list with estimated savings]."
