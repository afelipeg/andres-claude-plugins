# KPI Dictionary

## L1 — Strategic (C-Suite)

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-------|-----------|
| CAC | Total Marketing Spend / New Customers | CEO/CFO | Monthly |
| CLV | AOV x Retention Rate x Avg Months | CEO/CFO | Quarterly |
| CLV:CAC Ratio | CLV / CAC (target: >3:1) | CEO | Quarterly |
| Marketing ROI | (Revenue - Spend) / Spend | CFO | Monthly |
| Marketing Margin | (Revenue - Spend) / Revenue | CFO | Monthly |
| Market Share | Brand Revenue / Category Revenue | CEO | Quarterly |

## L2 — Tactical (Management)

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-------|-----------|
| CPA | Spend / Conversions | CMO | Weekly |
| ROAS | Revenue / Spend | CMO | Weekly |
| AOV | Revenue / Orders | CMO | Weekly |
| Conversion Volume | Total Conversions | CMO | Daily |
| Pipeline Value | Leads x Avg Deal Size x Win Rate | CMO | Weekly |
| Incremental ROAS | Incremental Revenue / Spend | CMO | Per test |

## L3 — Operational (Execution)

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency |
|-----|---------|-------|-----------|
| CPM | (Spend / Impressions) x 1000 | Media Team | Daily |
| CPC | Spend / Clicks | Media Team | Daily |
| CTR | Clicks / Impressions | Media Team | Daily |
| CVR | Conversions / Clicks | Media Team | Daily |
| Viewability | Viewable Impressions / Total | Media Team | Daily |
| Reach | Unique Users Reached | Media Team | Weekly |
| Frequency | Impressions / Reach | Media Team | Weekly |

## Translation Rules

- L3 -> L2: Aggregate channel metrics into campaign-level business metrics
- L2 -> L1: Connect campaign outcomes to financial impact using revenue bridge
- Always include time dimension (period-over-period comparison)
- Always flag data confidence level (actual vs estimated)
