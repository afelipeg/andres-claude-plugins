# C-Suite Report Templates

## CEO Summary Template

**Focus**: Market position, revenue growth, competitive advantage

```
MARKETING INVESTMENT SUMMARY — [Period]

Revenue Impact: $[X] attributed revenue from $[Y] investment
Customer Acquisition: [N] new customers at $[CAC] per customer
Customer Value: $[CLV] lifetime value (CLV:CAC = [X]:1)
Market Share: [X]% ([+/-] vs prior period)
Competitive Position: [Summary of SOV and competitive dynamics]

KEY DECISIONS NEEDED:
- [Decision 1 with revenue implication]
- [Decision 2 with market share implication]
```

## CFO Summary Template

**Focus**: ROI, efficiency, waste, P&L impact

```
MARKETING FINANCIAL REPORT — [Period]

Investment: $[X] total spend
Return: $[Y] attributed revenue | ROI: [X]%
Efficiency: [Score]/100
Marketing Margin: [X]%

WASTE ANALYSIS:
Productive Spend: $[X] ([Y]% of total)
Total Waste Identified: $[X]
Recoverable Savings: $[X] over [timeline]

ROAS COMPARISON:
Current ROAS: [X]
Productive ROAS (waste-adjusted): [Y]
Industry Benchmark: [Z]

P&L IMPACT:
[Line items affected with dollar amounts]
```
